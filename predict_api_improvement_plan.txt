Key Issues Identified:
──────────────────────

 A. Too generic / abstract
    - Predictions use "reflect", "progress", "balanced", "overall"
    - GT uses concrete: "6 PM", "grilled chicken", "Isha prayer", "Magelang"
    - The model strips out specific entities and replaces with summaries.

 B. Wrong tone / voice
    - Predictions sound like a formal writing coach
    - GT sounds like a student: informal, first-person, emotional
    - Examples: "it's what it's" vs "It made me think about..."

 C. Overuse of transition crutches
    - "Therefore,", "Overall,", "As a result,", "Afterward,"
    - These make predictions feel templated, not authentic.

 D. Sentence pattern dependency is too strict
    - When get_or_create_sentence_pattern() fails, the whole prediction
      fails (sids 9, 14, 17, 29 in first run)
    - The pattern sometimes forces unnatural structure

 E. No personal details from essay context
    - The essay contains specific details (times, places, names) but
      the prediction doesn't use them
    - PTM RAG and history snippets have rich context but it's not
      reflected in the output

 F. Writing style analysis is not effectively used
    - writing_patterns_formatted_output and writing_features_formatted_output
      are provided but the prompt doesn't force the model to mirror them
